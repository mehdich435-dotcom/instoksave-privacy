Privacy Policy – instokSave

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how instokSave handles information when you use the application. instokSave is an independent application and is not affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, ByteDance, or Meta.

instokSave does not require an account or sign-in. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, password, contacts, or precise location through the core functionality of the app.

When you paste a TikTok, Instagram or Facebook link into the app, the link is used to process your download request. Downloaded files and download history are intended to remain on your device.

You should only download content that you have permission or a legal right to download.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services required for advertising or app functionality. These services may process technical information such as device information, IP address, diagnostic information, or advertising identifiers according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. Network communications should use secure HTTPS connections where supported.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

instokSave does not intentionally maintain a server-side copy of downloaded media as part of the normal download feature. Download history stored locally by the app remains on your device and can be removed by deleting the relevant history or uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

instokSave is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the app.
